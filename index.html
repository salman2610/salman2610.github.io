How I work — six steps, from first recon to the retest.
01

Recon

Asset discovery, enumeration, technology fingerprinting. Understand what actually exists before deciding what to test.

Map

Attack surface mapping and threat modelling — what's reachable, what's exposed, and what actually matters if it breaks.

Test

Manual and tool-assisted testing against the OWASP Top 10 — Burp Suite and Nmap as a starting point, not the whole method.

Exploit

Validate real impact safely, in scope, with evidence. A finding isn't a finding until it's proven, not just flagged by a scanner.

Report

Every finding CVSS-scored and reproducible — a report your team can act on without a follow-up call to ask what it means.

Remediate

Fix guidance mapped to each finding, then a retest. A report nobody acts on was never worth writing.
